Verify your download

Trust, but check.

Every build's SHA-256 fingerprint is published here. If the hash of your file matches, it's exactly what we shipped.

    Open the APK in a file manager and choose Properties or Details → SHA-256, or use a checksum app from the Play Store. 

    shasum -a 256 bebu.apk
    certutil -hashfile bebu.apk SHA256
